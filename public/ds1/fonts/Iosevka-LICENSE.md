# Iosevka

`Iosevka-Regular.woff2` is Iosevka Extended Regular 33.3.6, cut down to the same 1,031 encoded characters as the previous Regular subset (Latin, punctuation, arrows, maths, box drawing). It is the font of `--typeface-mono`. The stable filename and CSS family alias are retained; the letterforms use the upstream Extended cut, with a 600-unit monospaced advance per 1,000-unit em rather than Regular's 500.

Source: `Iosevka-Extended.ttf` in the [official 33.3.6 TTF release](https://github.com/be5invis/Iosevka/releases/download/v33.3.6/PkgTTF-Iosevka-33.3.6.zip). Subset with FontTools, retaining the existing Unicode coverage and layout features, and encoded as WOFF2.
