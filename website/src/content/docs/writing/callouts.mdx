A callout sets a title and its body apart with an icon; folded, it shows only its title.

<Example name="callouts" />

## Try it

Choose Callout in the [slash menu](/editing/menus/), or type `> [!note]` and a space at the start of a block. The words on its first line are its title; Enter starts a line of its body under it. Press its icon, or press ⌘Enter with the caret in it, to fold it.

A callout stands a square in, its icon on the first square and a hairline at its start. Its title and body are formatted as any other words.

## The Markdown

| What you see | How you write it | What it stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A callout | Callout in the slash menu, or `> [!note]` and a space | `> [!note] Title`, then `> The body` on each line under it |
| A folded callout | Its icon, or ⌘Enter | `> [!note]- Title` |
| A toggle | Toggle in the slash menu | `> [!note]- Title` |
| A toggle heading | Toggle heading 1, 2 or 3 in the slash menu | `> [!note]- ## Title` |

The word in the brackets is the callout's type, any word in letters. Its icon follows it:

| Type | Icon |
| --- | --- |
| `[!tip]` | A tick |
| `[!warning]`, `[!caution]` | A box with a dot |
| `[!note]`, and any other | A box with lines |
| Any type, folded | A triangle |

Every line of a callout starts with `>`, so a Markdown reader that does not know callouts shows it as a quote.

## Folded callouts

<Example name="callouts-folded" />

A folded callout shows its title and hides its body. The body is kept as it was: folding and opening write or take away the one minus sign and nothing else. With the caret in a folded callout's body, the body is shown while it is edited. ⌘⌥T opens every folded callout in the editor.

A toggle is a folded callout made from the slash menu, the same Markdown.

## Toggle headings

<Example name="toggle-headings" />

A toggle heading is a callout whose title is a heading, folded to begin with: `#`, `##` or `###` and a space after the type make the title one. The title is set in the headings' medium face, and it folds and opens as any callout does.

## Keys

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Enter | A new line of the body |
| ⌘Enter | Folds the callout the caret is in, or opens it |
| ⌘⌥T | Opens every folded callout and toggle in the editor |

From code, `editor.toggle()` folds or opens the callout the caret is in, or ticks the box it is in, and `editor.toggle(true)` opens a callout. See [Methods and events](/building/methods/).
