A link is words with an address, edited through a field rather than its Markdown.

<Example name="links" />

## Try it

Select words and press ⌘K, or Link on the toolbar. A field for the address opens at the top of the editor: Apply writes the link, and Cancel or Escape leaves the words as they were. With the caret in a link, ⌘K opens the same field with its address in it, to change it. Clear the address and press Apply or Enter to remove the link while keeping its words and formatting.

Select words within one block and paste an address starting `https://` or `http://` over them, and the words become a link to it. ⌘⇧V pastes the address as plain text instead; selecting a link’s whole label also removes its old address.

## The Markdown

| What you see | How you write it | What it stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A link | ⌘K, Link on the toolbar, or an address pasted over words | `[words](https://example.com)` |
| An address | Type or paste it | `https://example.com` or `www.example.com`, as written |

An address in the text is a link as it stands: one starting `https://`, `http://` or `www.` is drawn as a link and kept exactly as it was written. A `www.` address opens as `https://`. Addresses in code and in a link's words stay as they are.

A link's words can be formatted like any others. Its address has no spaces, and parentheses in an address written through the field or pasted over words are stored as `%28` and `%29`, so the Markdown reads back as one link.

## Opening a link

<Example name="links-read-only" />

While the text can be changed, a press on a link puts the caret in it, as on any words. ⌘-press, or Ctrl-press, opens it in a new tab. In a read-only editor a press opens it in a new tab.

Links are underlined in the text's colour; `--aa-link` gives them a colour of their own. See [Styling](/building/styling/).

## Keys

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| ⌘K | Opens the address field for the selected words, or for the link the caret is in |
| ⌘V over selected words | Pasting an address makes the words a link |
| ⌘⇧V | Pastes as plain text |
| Escape in the field | Closes it without changing anything |
