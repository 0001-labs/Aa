Columns stand blocks side by side, each column written where it stands as a page of its own.

<Example name="columns-text" />

## Try it

Write in either column: Enter, Backspace, the keys and the slash menu work in a column as they do on the page. The columns share the measure, two squares apart. On a screen narrower than 480px they stand one under another.

## Making columns

<Example name="columns-pictures" />

Carry a block to the side of another, within its first or last fifth and no more than four squares in. A line down that side shows where it will stand; let go, and the two become columns, in the order they stand. Carried to the side of columns already there, a block becomes one more column. Columns are never carried into columns, and blocks carried together, several selected at once, are moved rather than put beside.

Columns in the [slash menu](/editing/menus/), or on a phone's bar, puts two empty columns where the caret is.

## Putting pictures in order

A block in a column is carried as any block is, up and down its column, and a picture lifts whichever way the press first moves. Carried over another column, a line shows down that column's nearer side; let go, and the block stands there as a column of its own. Pictures are put in order sideways this way, and the column it left goes when nothing is left in it. Each move is one step to undo.

How a block is lifted and carried is on [Selecting and moving](/editing/selecting/). Pictures in columns fill their column, a square apart down it.

## The Markdown

Columns are one block, written as Notion writes them: `<columns>` around a `<column>` for each, every column Markdown of its own.

```md
<columns>
<column>
## Before
The draft, as it was sent.
</column>
<column>
![A ceramic cup on a white table](cup.jpg)
</column>
</columns>
```

| What you see | How you write it | What it stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two columns | A block carried to another's side | `<columns>` holding two `<column>` |
| One more column | A block carried to the side of columns | Another `<column>` in the same `<columns>` |
| A column's writing | Write in the column | The column's Markdown between its tags |

The tags are never shown. Lines inside a column indented by tabs, as Notion indents them, are read without the tabs.

A change in a column is one change to the whole block, one step to undo, and the columns and the caret in them stay as they are. Files are the page's, not a column's: they land on the page beside the columns, and are carried in from there.
