Every paragraph and heading is a block of its own, and a heading is drawn at its size, never with its `#`.

<Example name="headings" />

## Try it

Type `#`, `##` or `###` and a space at the start of a paragraph and it becomes a heading of that level. Backspace at the start of a heading takes the level off, and it is a paragraph again. A heading with no words yet says "Heading 1", "Heading 2" or "Heading 3", since its `#` is not there to say it.

Enter ends a block where the caret is and starts the next; after a heading, the next block is a paragraph. ⌘⌥0 to ⌘⌥3 turn the caret's block into a paragraph or a heading, and so do Text and Heading 1 to 3 in the [slash menu](/editing/menus/).

## The Markdown

| What you see | How you write it | What it stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A title, 20px on two squares | `#` and a space, or ⌘⌥1 | `# Title` |
| A section, 17px | `##` and a space, or ⌘⌥2 | `## Section` |
| A part, 15px | `###` and a space, or ⌘⌥3 | `### Part` |
| A paragraph | Enter, or ⌘⌥0 | The words, with an empty line between blocks |

Levels 4 to 6, `####` to `######`, are read and kept, drawn at the text's size in the headings' medium face. Headings are drawn as Design system 1's prose draws them: a line a square, a square between blocks and two before a heading. `--aa-heading-gap` changes the room before a heading; see [Styling](/building/styling/).

## Lines in a paragraph

<Example name="paragraphs-lines" />

Shift-Enter breaks a line within a paragraph. The break is stored as one line break, and the lines stay one block: an empty line is what separates two blocks. Lines that follow each other in the Markdown, with no empty line between them, are one paragraph, drawn on lines of their own.

An empty block with the caret in it says "Type / for commands", so the slash menu is found without a button beside the block. `blockPlaceholder` changes it, and `''` leaves it out; see [Options](/building/options/).

## Keys

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Enter | Ends the block at the caret and starts the next |
| Shift-Enter | A line break within the block |
| Backspace at a heading's start | Makes it a paragraph |
| Backspace at a paragraph's start | Joins it to the block before |
| Delete at a block's end | Joins the next block to it |
| ⌘⌥0 | Text |
| ⌘⌥1, ⌘⌥2, ⌘⌥3 | Heading 1, 2 and 3 |
